What changed in the cyan ramp

Every stop moved on lightness only. Hue and saturation are untouched at all ten — this was not a desaturation. The pale end came down so it stops glowing against white, the dark end came up so it stops sinking into the dark canvas, and the middle interpolates between them. Values as of 14 Aug against what ships today.

Every stop, before and after

The ramp pivots around step 300, which did not move. Above it everything is slightly darker, below it slightly lighter — a gentle compression rather than a shift in one direction. Total lightness span went from 80.4 to 74.9.

The same colours in charts

Each row is one chart type drawn from the sequential scale — before on the left, after on the right. Switch the surface to see why the two ends moved in opposite directions.

Surface

The scale also lost its grey step. Its lightest cell used to borrow a neutral from the interface palette; it is the palest cyan now, so the whole scale is one hue. That is the visible difference in the treemap's “other” tile.

Where cyan sits among the other colours

Angle is hue, distance from the centre is chroma — how colourful something is. Every viz and status colour is plotted, so the palette can be read as one picture. Circles are viz, diamonds are status.

Cyan is one of the quietest hues we have

It peaks at chroma 39 — eighth of eleven viz families, against gold at 83 and pink at 74. The distance from the centre is the measure, and cyan sits well inside most of the palette. So the loudness was never saturation.

And it sits in the only clear arc

The categorical colours run out at teal, 192°, and pick up again at indigo, 286°. Cyan at 230° occupies a stretch nothing else claims, which is what lets a cyan primary appear beside any series without collision.

Which is why the hue did not move

Rotating toward blue takes it toward the blue used for links. Rotating toward green lands it 7° from the existing teal and, for red-green colourblind viewers, stops it separating from grey. The dashed arc marks where cyan can travel.

The four viz palettes

Charts draw from these four families and nothing else. Never hand-pick a hue primitive for a series, and never borrow status/* or text/* — those are tuned to contrast with the page, not with a neighbouring mark.
